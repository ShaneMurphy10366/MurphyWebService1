What we build

Most of our work falls into one of these. If yours doesn't, say so in the form and we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right shop.

A new website, start to finish

You get a site designed around what your customers actually come looking for — hours, prices, proof you're real, a way to get in touch. We write the pages with you, build them, and hand over a site you can edit yourself.

  • Custom design, no bought template
  • Written and organised with you, not around you
  • Editable pages so you're not stuck
  • Works on a five-year-old phone

Redesign of a site you already have

Keep what works, replace what doesn't. We audit the pages you have, keep your search rankings intact with proper redirects, and rebuild the rest.

Online stores

Product pages, checkout, shipping rules, and stock that stays in sync. Built on Shopify or a custom cart, depending on how many products you carry.

Booking and forms

Appointments, quote requests, waivers, intake forms. They land in your inbox or your calendar, and they don't get eaten by spam.

Getting found locally

Google Business Profile, map listings, service-area pages, and the structured data that makes your hours and reviews show up in search.

Speed and accessibility

A slow site loses people before they read a word, and an inaccessible one shuts customers out entirely. We test on real connections and with a screen reader before anything goes live.

How a project runs

Four steps, usually three to six weeks end to end. You always know which step you're in.

  1. 1

    A conversation

    Thirty minutes, no charge. What your business does, who you're trying to reach, and what the site has to accomplish. You leave with a plan whether or not you hire us.

  2. 2

    Layout and words

    We map the pages and draft the copy first, then design on top of it. You see the structure before anyone picks a colour, which is when changes are cheap.

  3. 3

    Build and review

    We build the real thing on a private link you can open any time. Two rounds of revisions are built in, and we don't count small fixes against them.

  4. 4

    Launch

    We connect your domain, set up analytics, check every form and link, and walk you through editing your own pages on a recorded call you can keep.

We don't disappear after launch

A website is not a thing you finish. Hosting expires, plugins break, hours change at the holidays, and somebody always needs a new page in a hurry. Care plans cover the boring parts so you're not chasing a freelancer who moved on.

How we build it

You don't need to care about any of this. It's here because you might want to ask.

Hosting
Deployed on Vercel's global network. Your site is served from whichever data centre is closest to the person opening it.
Speed
Pages ship as static files where possible, images sized and converted automatically, and nothing loads that isn't used.
Editing
A content editor made for people who don't write code. Change text, swap a photo, add a page.
Ownership
The domain, the code, and the accounts are yours. If you leave, you take everything with you.
Accessibility
Built to WCAG 2.2 AA — real contrast, keyboard navigation, and labels that make sense out loud.
Privacy
Analytics without third-party tracking cookies, so you don't need a consent banner in most cases.

Questions we get

How long does it take?

Three to six weeks for most sites, from the first call to launch. Stores and anything with booking logic run longer. The thing that slows projects down is almost always waiting on photos and text, so we'll pester you about those early.

Can I update it myself?

Yes, and we'd rather you did. Every site comes with an editor for text, images, and pages, plus a recording of us walking through it with your actual content.

Do I need to write everything?

No. We draft the copy and you correct it. You know your business better than we do, but a blank page is a bad place to start.

What if I already have a domain?

We'll use it. You keep the registration in your own name — we just point it at the new site and handle the certificate.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, photos if you have them, and about an hour of your time a week during the build. If you don't have decent photos, we'll tell you before it becomes a problem.

Do you work with businesses outside your area?

Yes. Most of the work happens over email and video calls regardless of where you are.

Fill this in and you'll hear back within one business day. If it turns out we're not the right fit, we'll say so and point you somewhere better.
